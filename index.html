The first step

“One must still have chaos in oneself to give birth to a dancing star.”

This is not a system to memorize. It is a dramatic experiment: a prophet who doubts his own prophecies, a teacher who asks to be abandoned, and a reader who must decide what deserves to be created.

Three constellations

Ideas to live
through—not agree with.

01

Übermensch

Self-overcoming

Not a superior biological type, but an image of the person who continually surpasses inherited limits and authors new values.

02

Eternal return

Radical affirmation

A test: if every joy, mistake, and ordinary afternoon returned forever, could you still say yes to your life as a whole?

03

Will to power

Creative force

Life does not merely preserve itself. It interprets, shapes, risks, transforms, and reaches beyond what it already is.

02

03
